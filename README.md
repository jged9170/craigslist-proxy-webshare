Three categories cover almost every legitimate Craigslist use case. Each has its place.

## What Most Beginners Get Wrong

A few traps worth naming so you don't fall into them.

Buying the cheapest plan and assuming it'll work for posting. The100-proxy datacenter plan at $2.99 is great for scraping, but you'll burn through Craigslist accounts trying to post with it. Pay for residential when posting maters.

Forgetting about browser fingerprinting. Even with a perfect proxy, if your browser leaks WebRTC, canvas, or font signals from the same machine across "different" accounts, Craigslist links them. Pair proxies with anti-detect browser if you're running multi-account.

Ignoring phone verification. Some Craigslist categories require PVA regardless of your IP. Buying proxies won't bypass that. Plan to source PVA numbers separately for those categories.

Rotating too aggressively. If your proxy switches IPs every 30 seconds during a single browsing session, that pattern looks robotic. Use sticky sessions on residential plans for human-like behavior.

Honestly, the proxy is one slice of the pie. The behavior layer maters just as much.
